Questions

About process and pricing

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes. The discovery call (30–40 minutes) and the one-page summary afterwards are free. There is no pitch on the call.

Why is the monthly price a range?

Because it depends on what your audit shows you need. Monthly plans start from ₹35,000 per month and the exact scope and price are agreed in writing before work starts.

What if our budget is lower?

Tell us your budget range on the discovery call. We will suggest a smaller plan with clearly named trade-offs, rather than a padded quote with a discount.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We are based in India and work with clients in India and abroad. Prices for international clients are agreed in writing in advance.
