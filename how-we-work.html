Questions

About process and pricing

Is the diagnosis really free?

Yes. The diagnosis call (30–40 minutes) and the one-page note afterwards are free. We show you your two biggest problems, and there is no pitch on the call.

What does a starting price mean?

It is where that product starts. After the free diagnosis we agree the exact scope and price in writing, before any work starts. Marketing Partner is priced on request.

What if our budget is lower?

Tell us your budget range on the discovery call. We will suggest a smaller scope with clearly named trade-offs, rather than a padded quote with a discount.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We are based in India and work with clients in India and abroad. Prices for international clients are agreed in writing in advance.
