The question nobody asks before selling you marketing

Why StartBox Ventures was built around one habit most agencies skip: finding out what is actually wrong before recommending anything. The story behind 'we diagnose before we prescribe'.

By StartBox Ventures4 October 20267 min read

The message

It usually arrives late in the evening, on WhatsApp, and it is almost always short.

"Hi. Can you run ads for us? Need more sales this month."

Most agencies read that as an order. A price goes back, a campaign goes live, money gets spent. A month later the owner is looking at a report full of reach and clicks, and the sales number has not moved. They try a different agency. Then another. Somewhere along the way they decide that "marketing doesn't work for a business like ours".

The ads were never the problem. Nobody stopped to ask what the problem was.

The pattern we kept seeing

StartBox Ventures began on 19 July 2024 with a simple, slightly uncomfortable observation: most businesses looking for marketing help had already been sold a solution before anyone asked what was wrong. More ads. A new website. A logo refresh. Another agency.

When we looked closely, the real causes were rarely the ones being paid for:

  • A beautiful website that explained what the business did, and never once asked the visitor to call.
  • Ads bringing hundreds of people to a page that did not mention the offer in the ad.
  • Enquiries arriving on WhatsApp and waiting four hours for a reply, while the customer messaged three competitors.
  • A strong product priced fairly, sitting inside a brand that made it look cheap, so every customer haggled.

None of these is fixed by spending more. Each is fixed by seeing it first.

What a good doctor does

Walk into a good doctor's clinic and say "I need antibiotics". They do not reach for the prescription pad. They ask where it hurts, how long, what changed. They examine. Only then do they write anything down, and sometimes what they write is "you don't need medicine, you need rest".

That is the whole idea behind StartBox, and it became our line: we diagnose before we prescribe. It sounds obvious. In marketing it is rare, because diagnosis is slow, it cannot be sold as a package, and it sometimes ends with us telling a business it does not need us.

The rules we wrote down

To keep ourselves honest, we turned the idea into a process with five steps and a few promises we do not bend:

  1. A free discovery call. Thirty to forty minutes, no pitch. By the end, you know the two most critical issues holding back your sales.
  2. A one-page summary. Free, useful whether or not you ever work with us.
  3. A full audit of your brand, website, social media, ads and sales process, written up in 48 hours.
  4. A roadmap session where you see a 90-day plan and exactly what each option costs.
  5. The fix, built and run by our team, with a clear report every month.

And the promises: nothing is recommended before we understand your business. No work starts before the price is agreed in writing. If we are not the right fit, we will tell you, and who is.

Where that has taken us

So far our work has come through referrals and direct outreach: people we have helped telling others, and conversations we started ourselves with businesses we believed we could help. Since July 2024 that has added up to more than 380 projects and 89 brands, for businesses in more than eight countries: D2C founders, B2B companies, coaches and experts, and local businesses through StartBox for MSME.

Ashmita Mishra, our founder, leads the strategy on every account. Shivani Tripathi, our CTO, leads technology and delivery. A team of designers, developers, video editors and ad specialists does the building. You always deal directly with us.

What it means for you

If you have tried ads, agencies and redesigns and still cannot say why sales are flat, the missing piece is probably not another service. It is a clear answer to one question: what is actually wrong?

That is the question we start with. Book a free discovery call and you will leave with your two biggest problems named, whatever you decide next. Or read how we work first.
