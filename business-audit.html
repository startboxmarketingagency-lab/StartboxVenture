What we look at

Five areas. One clear order of fixes.

Most audits look only at social media or only at ads. Ours follows the whole path from first glance to sale, because that is where the losses hide.

01 · Brand

Does it show your value?

Message, positioning and whether customers can see why you are worth the price.

02 · Website

Does it bring enquiries?

The first screen, the reasons to choose you, speed on mobile and the action you ask for.

03 · Social media

Does content lead anywhere?

What you post, where, and whether there is a path from content to a sale.

04 · Ads

Who are you paying to reach?

Targeting, the promise, the page it lands on and the cost of each real enquiry.

05 · Sales process

What happens after they enquire?

Reply speed, follow-up and the point where interested people drop off.
