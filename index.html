Sound familiar?

Most owners we meet say one of these.

The symptom is rarely the problem. Here is what is usually happening underneath.

01 / 06We're running ads, but sales aren't going up.

What is usually happening

The ads bring people in, but the offer, the page they land on or the follow-up loses them. More ads only make the loss bigger.

02 / 06People like our posts, but nobody buys.

What is usually happening

There is no clear path from seeing your content to paying you. That path is called a sales funnel, and it is missing.

03 / 06Our website looks fine, but no enquiries come from it.

What is usually happening

It explains what you do instead of why someone should choose you, and it never asks them to act.

04 / 06Customers say we're too expensive.

What is usually happening

Your brand doesn't show your value yet, so people compare you on price alone.

05 / 06Everything depends on me, or on referrals.

What is usually happening

Sales have no system behind them, so growth stops the moment you do.

06 / 06We want to sell online, but don't know where to start.

What is usually happening

You need the right pieces in the right order: offer, website, payments, then marketing.

That's why we never start with ads. We start by finding the real problem.
