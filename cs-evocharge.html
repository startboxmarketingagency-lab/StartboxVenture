Who they are and what they are building

"Fleet operators do not want chargers. They want guaranteed vehicle uptime. EvoCharge must sell the outcome, not the hardware."

EvoCharge is operating at the intersection of EV Charging Infrastructure and a rapidly changing commercial environment. Founded by a team with deep domain expertise, the company has built a product that genuinely solves a real problem — but has not yet built the commercial engine to match the product quality.

The core tension: the team's instinct is to build better technology. The market's requirement is for better storytelling, better positioning, and a repeatable sales motion that does not depend on the founder's personal network.

Market research and brand audit

"The market is larger than the obstacle. The question is always: which lever moves first?"

The EV Charging Infrastructure sector ($140B — EV charging infrastructure market by 2030) is undergoing structural transformation. EvoCharge's competitive position is strong on product quality and weak on commercial visibility.

The competitor analysis reveals a positioning window: the premium segment of the EV Charging Infrastructure market is occupied by legacy providers whose products are aging and whose pricing has become indefensible. EvoCharge has the technology to challenge them — the job is to build the brand authority to justify the conversation.

Strategy &
Positioning

Marketing strategy, brand story, UVP and channel mix

"We do not compete on features. We compete on the outcome we guarantee."

Positioning: EvoCharge should own the outcome-first positioning in EV Charging Infrastructure — leading every conversation with a specific, measurable result that existing providers cannot credibly claim.

Channel mix: Content-led demand generation (40%), research-led direct outbound (35%), referral and partner activation (25%). Paid acquisition held until organic channels demonstrate repeatable product-market fit.

Brand Promise / UVP
The best outcome in EV Charging Infrastructure. Proven. Ask our references.

10 / 10 — StartBox Verdict

If StartBox were hired as Growth Readiness Partner — these are our top five priorities.

01

Define the category before a competitor does

The positioning window in EV Charging Infrastructure is 12–18 months. After that, the market forms around whoever moved first. StartBox recommends moving in the first 30 days.

02

Make existing customers the primary growth engine

The most efficient path to revenue is through buyers who already trust you. A structured referral programme will generate 40% of Year 2 revenue from Year 1 customers.

03

Build the content engine before scaling paid acquisition

Content that demonstrates expertise generates compound returns. Every article continues generating leads for years. Build the asset before the channel.

04

Hire commercial talent with domain credibility, not just sales skills

Buyers will not engage with salespeople who do not understand their world. The next commercial hire must have operated in the sector — not just sold into it.

05

Set a 90-day revenue target and work backwards from it

Strategy without a revenue target is philosophy. Set a specific, non-negotiable 90-day revenue number and reverse-engineer the activity required to hit it.
