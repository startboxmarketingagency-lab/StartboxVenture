Questions

Common questions

Which platform should we focus on first?

The one your buyers already use to decide. For many B2B businesses that is LinkedIn; for D2C brands it is usually Instagram and YouTube. The audit settles it with your data, not trends.

Do you shoot the content too?

We plan, script, edit and post. Shooting can be done by you on a phone with our guidance, or arranged separately.

Do you work with coaches and experts outside India?

Yes. We are based in India and a large part of our coaching and expert work is with clients in other countries.
