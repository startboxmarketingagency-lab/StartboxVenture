Questions

Common questions

What does a monthly growth partner actually do?

Every month our founder works with you on your numbers: what is selling, what is leaking, what to try next and who does it. You get decisions, not decks.

What kind of automation do you build?

Things like website forms that flow straight into your sheet or CRM, WhatsApp follow-ups, order and inventory data synced to your site, and simple internal tools.

Do we need to change our software?

Usually not. We start by connecting what you already use, and only recommend new tools when the old ones are the problem.
