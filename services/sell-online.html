Common questions

Should I start with my own website or with Amazon and Flipkart?

It depends on your product, margins and how customers already find you. That is exactly what the audit answers, before you spend on either.

Do you manage marketplace listings after setup?

We set up and optimise listings as a project, and can run them as part of a monthly plan if you need ongoing management.

Yes: the offer, the course page, payments and the launch marketing. We start by checking that people will actually pay for the topic.
