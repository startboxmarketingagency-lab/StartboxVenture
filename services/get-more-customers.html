No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta from your account, so you always see exactly what is spent. Our fee covers strategy, creatives, setup and management.

Short video ads produced faster and at lower cost using AI tools, then edited by our team to your brand. Useful for testing many angles before investing in a big shoot.
