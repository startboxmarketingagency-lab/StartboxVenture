The Client

Not just an AI company. Not just IoT. Not just a research lab.

Adhyava Research & Technologies — an early-stage deep tech company with AI-powered road asset monitoring, computer vision, IoT sensors, and a flagship product called iRAMA. Excellent product. No commercial traction yet.

The Problem

Not a product problem. Not a technology problem.

They were speaking the language of researchers to an audience of project directors and CEOs. The translation was missing. Private EPC companies don't buy research — they buy risk reduction and proof.

The Brief

Not branding. Not a campaign. Not a content calendar.

A full go-to-market strategy targeting private infrastructure companies — EPC contractors and PMCs executing India's national highway buildout. Stage: early. Goal: first pipeline in 90 days.

Posi­tion­ing

Naming the category before a competitor could

"Features get compared on price. Categories get owned."

The single most important decision: stop calling Adhyava an "AI solutions company" and position it as a Road Intelligence company. One category claim. Specific. Defensible. Immediately legible to a buyer who builds roads for a living. Then we built who to sell to — and in what order.

Brand Promise — One Line
"We turn India's roads into data — so infrastructure companies build safer, win more, and prove it."
Tier 01
Win First

Mid-sized EPC Road Contractors

₹500Cr–₹5,000Cr turnover. Active on NHAI projects. Under pressure to demonstrate safety compliance. Reachable, decision-fast, hungry for digital differentiation.

→ Dilip Buildcon · PNC Infratech · KNR Constructions
Tier 02
Scale Into

Project Management Consultants

Technical advisors hired by NHAI and state bodies. If they recommend iRAMA, demand cascades downstream to every EPC client they supervise.

Harvest Later

Long sales cycles. Massive contract sizes. Approach only after 3–5 Tier 1 reference wins are in hand. These names validate the category; they don't start it.

→ L&T Infra · Tata Projects · Adani Road

Strat­egy

Four moves that compound, not cancel

"In a trust-driven sector, every channel must earn its cost before we recommend it."

We designed a GTM built on proof, not promotion. Private infra buyers are technically sophisticated and commercially cautious. You earn them with data about their projects — not ads about yours. One firm rule: no free pilots. Even a nominal fee signals commercial seriousness and makes the buyer take the relationship seriously.

Channel priority verdict: Direct outbound with research-led hooks · Thought leadership as pipeline · PMC partner channel · Paid digital held until Month 9.

Research-Led Outbound

"We analysed 42km of roads like your Rajkot stretch — want the findings?" leads to meetings no cold pitch can. Adhyava's research engine is the unfair advantage.

Priority 01
Thought Leadership as Pipeline

Annual India Road Intelligence Report. School Zone Safety Index by city. The goal: most-cited source in road safety data in India within 18 months.

Priority 02
PMC Partner Channel

One PMC relationship unlocks their entire client portfolio. Co-branded reporting program makes recommending iRAMA easy and natural.

Priority 03
Three-Product Commercial Model

Entry: Road Intelligence Snapshot (₹2–8L). Core: iRAMA platform license (₹15–50L/year). Anchor: Safety Intelligence Partnership (₹75L–₹2Cr).
